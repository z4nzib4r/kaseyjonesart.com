Hello! My name is Kasey Jones, and I am a multimedia artist and designer. My creative output spans static two-dimensional works intended to transform a space or tell a story, large-scale murals, three-dimensional installations, and interactive art experiences. I work across media because versatility is fundamental to my practice.
