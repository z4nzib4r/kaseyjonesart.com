Ember Study is a wall of thermochromic panels that hold the heat signatures of the people who approach them. Stand close and the surface blooms from near-black to rust and orange; step away and the color fades over several minutes, like a coal losing its glow.

![The panel wall mid-exhibition](./01.jpg)

Sensors track proximity rather than touch, so the wall responds to presence itself — hesitation reads differently than confidence, a crowd differently than a single visitor.

![Detail of a panel responding to a visitor](./02.jpg)

The record is temporary but never quite empty: on busy days the wall carries a faint accumulated warmth, a residue of everyone who came before.

![The wall cooling at the end of the day](./03.jpg)
