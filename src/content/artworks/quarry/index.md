Quarry is a twelve-channel sound installation staged in a decommissioned granite quarry. Field recordings made during the quarry's final working year — drills, saws, blasting horns, lunch whistles — were slowed, stretched, and returned to the space that made them.

![The quarry floor with speaker stones](./01.jpg)

Speakers hidden among the cut stone play the material at geological speed: a single blast lasts twenty minutes, a workday lasts the full season. Visitors describe the effect as hearing the quarry remember itself.
