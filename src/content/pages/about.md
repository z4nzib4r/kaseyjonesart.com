Hello! My name is Kasey Jones, and I am a multimedia artist and designer. My journey as an artist began in childhood and has taken root throughout nearly four decades of my life. I began drawing and painting in primary school, winning a drawing contest in kindergarten at the Butler Institute of American Art in Youngstown, Ohio. My mother encouraged my creativity, and as a result, I learned to encourage and trust it in myself.

My formal education exposed me to the foundations of art production, but it wasn't until I began painting large-scale theatrical backdrops during my undergraduate studies at Ohio University that I started to realize the possibilities of what my creative practice could become.

As a former collegiate athlete, I have also learned to lean into my competitive nature as a means of honing my creative voice and eye. That drive gives my practice an edge. I have a strong sense of composition and am typically drawn to bold, high-contrast color, rhythm, and pattern. As I have developed my eye for design, I have simultaneously developed my voice as an artist. I am adept at creating personal work that resonates with broader themes while remaining rooted in my own lived experiences.

My creative output spans static two-dimensional works intended to transform a space or tell a story, large-scale murals, three-dimensional installations, and interactive art experiences. I work across media because versatility is fundamental to my practice. I am interested in the idea first and allow the concept to determine the medium, rather than limiting an idea to a particular material or discipline.

I have often been described as a "Renaissance woman," a title that reflects both the breadth of my interests and my desire to continually learn, experiment, and make. Whether I am painting, designing, building, composing, performing, or creating an immersive experience, the thread connecting my work is the same: a strong creative instinct, an intentional eye, and the drive to see an idea through.
