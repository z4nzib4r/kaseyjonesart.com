Creating Healthy Communities (CHC), a program of the Athens City-County Health Department, collaborated with the Village of Amesville and local artist Kasey Jones on an asphalt art installation at the intersections of four village streets.

Asphalt art encourages drivers to slow down and be more alert for pedestrians and cyclists, the most vulnerable users of the road. Asphalt art is also a placemaking strategy, whereby community members create and revitalize public spaces.

The murals encourage peaceful streets while highlighting a point of community pride: the Amesville Elementary Running Club. Last year the students logged over 7,000 miles. Painted murals decorate the four corners of the running club's route.

This project is one of a suite of pedestrian safety projects that CHC has worked on with the village, including the adoption of a complete streets policy and an upcoming ODOT grant-funded project to implement traffic calming along State Routes 550 and 329.
