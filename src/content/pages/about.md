I am a multimedia, visual, and performative artist and designer. My practice spans a wide range of media, from visual art and design to composing and performing music. My process follows the path of least resistance. As a mother of three, my practice ebbs and flows with the rhythms of my life; the medium may change and the scale may shift, but I am always creating.

I am a go-getter. I have ideas, and I see them through. I have a strong sense of composition and push a design until my eye tells me it is right. Color, high contrast, rhythm, and pattern are constants in my work, creating something striking, immersive, and distinctly my own.
