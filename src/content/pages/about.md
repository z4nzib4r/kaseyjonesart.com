![Kasey Jones, photographed outdoors against green summer foliage](./kasey-jones.jpg)

Kasey Jones is an interdisciplinary artist based in the United States. She holds a BFA in Art History from Ohio University and an MFA in Community Arts from the Maryland Institute College of Art (MICA). She has exhibited in solo and group shows across the country, and her work is held in private collections internationally.

Her practice is grounded in intentional design — leading the field and pushing compositions in new and unexpected ways. Light, color, and contrast are elements she is drawn to as a way to draw people into the visual space. She loves ideation and the beginning stages of design, thinking up the big idea and then seeing it through with a precise, focused eye.

Her approach is rooted in the exploration of material, and she creates large-scale, participatory and public projects alongside personal work that responds to the social and environmental injustices that impact her life and the lives of others. By having an open-ended view of what materials she can use, her art making process is constantly being pushed by the varying limitations each material may have. Her exploration of media has led her to develop bodies of work in murals, sculpture, painting, photography, living sculptures, audio, video, music, and textiles.
