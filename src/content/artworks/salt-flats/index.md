Salt Flats brings three tons of harvested salt into the gallery, raked into a shallow terraced field that visitors cross on a narrow steel walkway. Over the run of the exhibition, a slow drip system dissolves and recrystallizes the surface, so the terrain no two weeks looks the same.

![The terraced salt field at opening](./01.jpg)

By the closing weeks the field has developed ridgelines and fractures that no one designed. The piece is documented weekly; the full sequence is part of the work.
