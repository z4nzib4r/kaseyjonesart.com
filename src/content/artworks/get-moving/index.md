*Get Moving* is a laser-cut sheet metal installation in the stairwells of the Ohio University College of Osteopathic Medicine, made to encourage people to take the stairs. The figures are installed throughout the building's four-level stairwell, running up the wall alongside the steps and shifting from dark reds and blues into yellow and green as they climb.

![The stairwell seen from a landing, cut metal figures striding across the white tiled wall above and below the orange handrails](./01.jpg)

The commission came out of a residency working with students at Federal Hocking High School. We began with Eadweard Muybridge's motion studies — the sequential photographs that break a stride into single frames, and that led the way to moving pictures — and used them to understand how a body actually carries itself through a run. From there we worked through color theory. The students learned to depict the figure, and to mix a gradient that moves cleanly from one color into the next.

The color and movement studies for the commission, worked out on paper first.

![Painted paper panels layered edge to edge, cut figures in motion pinned across them, a label in the corner reading "OU COM Commission, Color Studies and Movement Studies"](./02.jpg)

We drew the figures to scale, cut them out of sheet metal, primed them, and painted them.
