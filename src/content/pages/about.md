As an interdisciplinary artist my approach is rooted in the exploration of material, and I create large-scale, participatory and public projects alongside personal work that responds to the social and environmental injustices that impact my life and the lives of others. By having an open-ended view of what materials I can use, my art making process is constantly being pushed by the varying limitations each material may have. My exploration of media has led me to develop bodies of work in murals, sculpture, painting, photography, living sculptures, audio, video, music, light and textiles.

Whatever the medium, my practice is grounded in intentional design. I am looking at composition, color, line, and contrast in conjunction with content, and at how people will move through, interact with, and visually experience the work. I love ideation and the beginning stages of design — thinking up the big idea — and then seeing it through with a precise, focused eye.

My current gallery work focuses on the injustices I have faced through my experiences of being a woman and a mother in contemporary society. The way we treat women, especially mothers, reflects what we value the most and what we value the least. Women and mothers alike still face gender discrimination, gender pay gap within their respective fields of work, and gender stereotypes, to name a few. It is my hope that my artistic voice will resonate with the millions of women who still experience such oppression. We are not alone in this journey together and it is my ultimate goal to use my creativity as a means to unite people in support of the women and mothers of the world.

Kasey Jones is an interdisciplinary artist based in the United States. She holds a BFA in Art History and an MFA in Community Arts from the Maryland Institute College of Art (MICA), a foundation that continues to shape the participatory, community-rooted side of her practice. She has exhibited in solo and group shows across the country, and her work is held in private collections internationally.
