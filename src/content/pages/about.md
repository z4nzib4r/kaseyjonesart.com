![Kasey Jones, photographed outdoors against green summer foliage](./kasey-jones.jpg)

I transform the ordinary into the visually extraordinary through intentional design. I am a multimedia, visual, and performative artist with a strong eye for composition. Using color, high contrast, rhythm, and pattern, I create work that is striking and immersive. Whether working in two or three dimensions, my goal is to create artwork that is both approachable and impactful.

My interdisciplinary practice spans murals, sculpture, painting, photography, textiles, installation, light, video, sound, and public art. Whether exhibited in a gallery or integrated into the public landscape, my work is designed to engage people both physically and visually.

I am also a musician, classically trained on more than four instruments, and a thespian who acted all through childhood and into college. I am an organizer, a business owner, and an entrepreneur. Life is an art, and I approach all of it with the eye, the critique, and the precision of someone who stays curious about what can be actualized.

My process follows the path of least resistance. I am a mother of three young children, and my practice ebbs and flows the way my days do when I am looking after them, but I am always creating in some way.

I am a go-getter. I have ideas and I see them through. I push a design based on what my eye tells me: whether it makes the point clearly, whether it is clever, whether the color choices and the high contrast make something worth looking at. It has to be my own. My work will always carry that Kasey Jones flair.
