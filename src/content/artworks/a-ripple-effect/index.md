[National Aquarium](https://aqua.org/)

Commissioned by the National Aquarium for Artscape, *A Ripple Effect* is a low-relief map of the Chesapeake Bay fabricated entirely from single-use plastic drinking straws.

I used more than 125,000 straws, hot-glued to a plexiglass and wood frame and lit with programmable LED lights.
