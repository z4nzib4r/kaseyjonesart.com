Blue Hour is an immersive light environment that stretches the fifteen minutes after sunset into a full hour. Visitors enter through a corridor of layered scrim, and the room's color temperature drifts almost imperceptibly from amber toward deep blue.

![Installation view of Blue Hour](./01.jpg)

The fog field responds to movement: where people gather, the air clears slightly, opening pockets of visibility that close again as they move on. A low ambient score, tuned to the light cycle, marks the passage without ever announcing it.

![Detail of the layered scrim wall](./02.jpg)

The work asks how attention changes when a transition we normally ignore becomes the entire event. Most visitors stay far longer than they intend to.
