Night Garden is an outdoor installation that only fully exists after dark. By day it reads as an unremarkable planted bed; at night, channels of bioluminescent algae trace the irrigation lines, and low solar fixtures pick out the undersides of leaves.

![The garden an hour after sunset](./01.jpg)

The piece was planted with species that close, fold, or release scent at night, so the garden's night self is genuinely different from its day self — not just lit differently, but behaving differently.
