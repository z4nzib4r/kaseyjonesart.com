I am an interdisciplinary artist based in the United States. I hold a BFA in Art History from Ohio University and an MFA in Community Arts from the Maryland Institute College of Art (MICA). I have exhibited in solo and group shows across the country, and my work is held in private collections internationally.

My practice is grounded in intentional design — leading the field and pushing compositions in new and unexpected ways. Light, color, and contrast are elements I am drawn to as a way to draw people into the visual space. I love ideation and the beginning stages of design, thinking up the big idea and then seeing it through with a precise, focused eye.
